Who we are

Built in Queensland for real family life

Applepie Entertainment Pty Ltd is an Australian digital product company based in Queensland. We create clear, encouraging software for children and the grown-ups who support them.

Our current product, Mission Minis, brings routines, learning activities and family rewards together in one playful, parent-managed experience.

Our product

Mission Minis

Mission Minis helps children work through everyday routines, practise age-appropriate learning and save stars towards family-chosen rewards. Parents remain in control of profiles, activities, timers and purchases.

  • No advertising or behavioural tracking
  • No child accounts, chat or public profiles
  • Family activity information stays on the device
  • Grown-up controls are protected by a Parent PIN
