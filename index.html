Mission Minis

A playful family app that helps children build routines, learn new skills and work towards rewards they care about.

Coming to iPhone, iPad and Android.

Everyday jobs become mini adventures

Parents set the plan. Children complete friendly missions, collect stars and see their progress grow.

Routines that feel achievable

Morning routines, clean-up time, dinner preparation, washing and brushing are broken into clear steps.

Learning with momentum

Maths, words, clocks, problem solving and tracing missions build confidence a little at a time.

Rewards with meaning

Stars can go towards small family treats or larger goals, helping children practise patience and saving.

Made for families, with parents in control

Mission Minis is designed for young users without turning their family life into an online profile.

  • No advertising or behavioural tracking
  • No child accounts, chat or public profiles
  • Routine and learning information stays on the device
  • Parent controls are protected by a grown-up PIN
